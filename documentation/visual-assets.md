### Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: finished website hero illustration for GAP, Global Admission Platform, a study-abroad guidance service. Create one premium editorial illustration, landscape 3:2 composition, on warm ivory #fbfbf8. Sculptural paper-cut and softly textured 3D collage: a large deep forest-green globe with fine understated latitude lines, a small cream university building, an open study notebook, a passport without any identifying text, and a small gold airplane tracing a graceful curved path. Restrained forest-green #174b3a, pale sage #e8eee7, muted gold #d7a64a palette. Beautiful natural shadows, sophisticated tactile material, warm daylight, generous breathing room. The composition should read as a single coherent inviting study-abroad scene, not scattered stock icons. No humans, no text, no logos, no watermark, no UI, no buttons, no border. Keep all important objects safely within the central 85% for responsive cropping.
